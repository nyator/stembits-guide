A band in your pocket. Loops, pads and a click that play with you.

How it worksOne bar, four beats

Set it up once. Then just play.

  1. Beat 1

    Set up a session

    Pick the loops you need for your gig, service or practice and save them together.

  2. Beat 2

    Hear the beat

    Turn on the click and put it in one ear, so you keep time and the room never hears it.

  3. Beat 3

    Add loops and pads

    Tap a loop for a groove, or a pad for a soft held sound underneath. Tap again to stop.

  4. Beat 4

    Play along

    Grab your guitar, keys or mic and perform over it.

Inside the appSix tabs

Everything has its own place.

  • Session

    Your set for the night. Gather the loops you need for a gig, service or rehearsal and keep them together in one place.

    • Build a setPick loops from your library and save them as a session you can reopen any time.
    • Volume per loopSet how loud each loop sits, so the groove never drowns you out.
    • Pan per loopSend each loop left, right or centre.
    • Click per sessionSwitch the click on or off for each session, with its own volume and pan.
    • Ableton LiveControl your Live sets and setlists right from your phone.

  • Loops

    Your library of drum and groove loops.

    • BrowseSorted by style (Worship, Praise, Funk) and by artist.
    • Import your ownBring in loops from your phone and use them just like the built-in ones.
    • Instant startLoops are ready before you tap, so there is no waiting mid-song.
    • No gapsEvery loop repeats cleanly, bar after bar.
    • Change the speedSlow a loop down or push it faster and the pitch stays where it is.

  • Pads

    Soft, held sounds that fill the space under your playing.

    • Any keyChoose the key of the song and the pad follows.
    • Smooth changesMove to a new key and the pads fade across, with no clicks or jumps.
    • Made for the in-betweenPrayer, transitions, intros and quiet moments.

  • Click

    A steady metronome that keeps everyone together.

    • TempoSet the speed in beats per minute.
    • Time signaturesPlay in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 and more, with beat one accented so you always know where the bar starts.
    • Volume and panTurn it up or down, and pan it so it lives only in your earpiece.
    • Rock solidThe click runs on its own engine, so it stays in time even while loops and pads play.

  • Store

    New sounds for your library.

    • PacksBrowse and download new loops and packs.
    • Always growingNew packs appear in the store as they are released.

  • Settings

    Make the app yours.

    • AccountManage your sign-in and profile.
    • PreferencesSet the app up the way you like to work.
Good to knowBefore your first gig

A few tips from the stage.

  • Click in your ear only

    Pan the click hard left and the music hard right. Then use a stereo splitter cable (one 3.5 mm plug to two outputs) and send the left side to your earpiece and the right side to the speakers. You hear the click, the room hears the band.

  • Build sessions ahead

    Set up a session for each gig or service the day before, with volumes and pans already balanced. On the night you just open it and play.

  • Count yourself in

    Start the click a bar before the loop so you and the team feel the tempo before the groove drops.
