Stembits

A band in your pocket. Loops, pads and a click that play with you.

92 BPMTap the bar to hear the click
How it worksOne bar, four beats

Set it up once. Then just play.

  1. Beat 1

    Set up a session

    Pick the loops you need for your gig, service or practice and save them together.

  2. Beat 2

    Hear the beat

    Turn on the click and put it in one ear, so you keep time and the room never hears it.

  3. Beat 3

    Add loops and pads

    Tap a loop for a groove, or a pad for a soft held sound underneath. Tap again to stop.

  4. Beat 4

    Play along

    Grab your guitar, keys or mic and perform over it.

Inside the appSix tabs

Everything has its own place.

  • Session

    Build your own set from the loops you want for a gig, service or rehearsal. Switch the click on or off per session and send it left, right or centre. You can also control your Ableton Live sets and setlists from here.

  • Loops

    Drum and groove loops sorted by style (Worship, Praise, Funk) and by artist. They start the moment you tap and repeat with no gap. Change the speed and the pitch stays put.

  • Pads

    Soft, held sounds in any key that fade smoothly from one to the next. Made for prayer, transitions and intros.

  • Click

    A steady metronome. Set your speed and pan it so it lives only in your earpiece.

  • Store

    Browse and download new loops and packs for your library.

  • Settings

    Manage your account and set the app up the way you like it.

Church service

The drummer can't make it on Sunday. The worship leader opens the team's session, sends the loops to the speakers and keeps the click in her earpiece.

Practice at home

Ama is learning a new song on keys. She starts a drum loop, slows it to a comfortable speed and plays until it sticks.

Jamming

A producer taps a few pads, layers a beat and some chords, and writes ideas over the top.
